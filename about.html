    • About Manny's Rentals

    Manny's Rentals is a rental dealership that was founded in 1967 By James Blake which is meant for people visiting KZN and for people that simply need a source of transportation.

    "Cars have always had a spot in my heart. But to be honest, my dad was the person that got me into loving them when i helped him rebuild a car and sell a Honda Civic Type R."

    Rental policy: -Customers over the age of 18 are allowed to rent a vehicle. -A license is required to rent a vehicle. -All tickets recieved while renting the vehicle will be the drivers responsibility to take care of.

    Manny was born in 1970 and grew up around cars.
